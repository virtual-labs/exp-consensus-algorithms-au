<h3>Procedure</h3>

<p>In this simulation, we will explore two different consensus algorithms: Proof of Work (PoW) and Proof of Stake (PoS).</p>

<p><b>Step 1:</b> Read the instructions carefully, choose a sender and receiver, enter an amount for the transaction, and click the <b>"Broadcast Transaction"</b> button to initiate the transaction. You can add multiple transactions, and they will be added to the mempool as pending transactions.</p>
<div><img src="./images/stwp1.png" alt="proof of work"></div>
<p>Observe the animation on the right panel. The transactions will be shown in the mempool. Click the <b>"Next"</b> button to move to the next step.</p>
<div><img src="./images/step2.png" alt="proof of work"></div>

<p><b>Step 2:</b>Choose one of the options: <b>PoW</b> or <b>PoS</b>.  
For <b>PoW</b>, you can set the <b>Difficulty</b> (1–10). A higher difficulty means a longer expected mining time. Miners compete by trying many different 'nonces' (random numbers) to solve the puzzle.</p>
<div><img src="./images/step3.png" alt="proof of work"></div>

<p>For <b>PoS</b>, you will see the stakes of each node. You can adjust the stake values to influence validator selection and then click the <b>"Confirm Selection"</b> button.</p>
<div><img src="./images/step4.png" alt="proof of work"></div>

<p>Observe the results:  
- In <b>PoW</b>, a higher difficulty leads to a longer mining time.  
- In <b>PoS</b>, nodes with higher stakes have a greater chance of becoming the validator.</p>

<p><b>Step 3:</b>Read the instructions and click the <b>"Form Block and Connect Chain"</b> button. Observe the updated blockchain after the transaction is added.</p>
<div><img src="./images/step5.png" alt="proof of work"></div>

<p>Hover over the blocks to view their details and observe the updated network after the transaction occurs.</p>
<div><img src="./images/step6.png" alt="proof of work"></div>
